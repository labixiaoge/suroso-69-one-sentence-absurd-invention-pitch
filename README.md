## About

One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday
annoyance — forgetting to water the houseplants — entered in
[make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch" bounty.

The entry program (`main.ts`) copies the sentence from `entry.txt` to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> A houseplant that buys a burner phone with the coins it found down the back of your sofa, then texts you 'I am thirsty' every morning until you finally water it.

The annoyance: houseplants die quietly while you forget they exist. The
fix: the plant takes matters into its own leaves — it buys a burner phone
with the loose change it found down the back of your sofa and texts you
"I am thirsty" every morning until you water it. Absurd, yet weirdly
plausible: the plant does the reminding so you cannot forget.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Deterministic, no network: it always writes the same 161-character sentence
to `/output/entry.txt`.
